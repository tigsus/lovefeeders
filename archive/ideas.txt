## Homepage Narrative (Complete Draft)

### 1. **Opening Hook**

**Headline:** *“Name a nonprofit that desires to cease operations?”*
**Body:**
Lovefeeders exists not to last forever, but to make itself unnecessary.

---

### 2. **Why Then Are We Still Alive?**

The disruption has yet to fully hit the world. AI and robotics are about to unleash unprecedented productivity gains — but without a new framework, the fallout will be destructive. Lovefeeders is here to channel that disruption into constructive change:

* **Inability to sustain the technology** that enables increased productivity ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2025/explained-generative-ai-environmental-impact-0117), [AIBusiness](https://aibusiness.com/responsible-ai/the-promise-of-ai-is-at-risk-if-sustainability-isn-t-a-key-consideration-in-2025))
* **Mass unemployment** as traditional labor markets shrink ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full-potential-at-work))
* **Social instability** driven by economic displacement and unrest ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jackkelly/2025/04/25/the-jobs-that-will-fall-first-as-ai-takes-over-the-workplace/))

---

### 3. **What Is the New Normal?**

AI and robotics can take over work, but humans still need value and purpose. Surplus productivity requires human stewardship.

The classical definition of productive labor must expand. While disruption may fracture old definitions, the new normal will settle on an expanded view — where human labor is not erased but redefined and measured through **Abundance Metrics**.

---

### 4. **What Are Abundance Metrics?**

Abundance Metrics expand the definition of labor. They measure contributions in:

* **Service** — caregiving, volunteering, community support
* **Education** — learning, teaching, knowledge sharing
* **Wholeness** — mental, cultural, and environmental health

They make invisible work visible, giving policymakers, funders, and communities the data to guide resilience.

---

### 5. **Getting Paid for an Abundance Activity?**

Yes. Without redefining value, the risks are clear: broken technology systems, mass unemployment, and social instability.

Capturing Abundance Metrics ensures governments and organizations have the evidence to **fund, incentivize, and reward** contributions beyond traditional labor.

---

### 6. **From Pilots to Policy**

Lovefeeders’ projects are not just humanitarian — they are **live laboratories** testing how Abundance Metrics can be applied in real-world settings.

These pilots generate statistical evidence that funders, NGOs, and policymakers can use to shape economic planning in the age of AI and robotics:

* **USA-Feeders 2030** — piloting digital pantries as resilience hubs, logging redirected time, education completions, and service hours.
* **Honduras: City of Refuge** — digitizing food networks to track efficiency gains and well-being improvements.
* **South Sudan: City of Angels** — integrating AI education with community-building, measuring abundance uplift across displaced populations.

**Why it matters:** These projects create data streams that can be scaled into **CSR reporting, grant accountability, and policy frameworks**, proving that Abundance Metrics are more than theory — they are measurable, auditable, and ready for integration.

---

### 7. **What Are the Deliverables?**

Lovefeeders is not just a theory shop — we produce frameworks, tools, and public goods that others can use, adopt, and improve upon.

**Existing Deliverables**

* [**Value Repositioning Framework (VRF)**](#) — expanding the classical definition of labor value through Abundance Metrics.
* [**Societal Standard of Living (SSOL)**](http://societalstandardofliving.com/) — a dynamic model for ensuring wages and self-sufficiency align with real costs of living.

**Planned Deliverables**

* **Free Business Applications** — accounting, inventory, GIS mapping, and vulnerability assessment tools available to the public.
* **Audits / KPI / VRF Reporting** — transparent systems for tracking abundance contributions and project impact.
* **Video Documentaries** — capturing starting points, struggles, and successes, providing learning material for communities and policymakers alike.

---

### 8. **Would You Help Us Shut Down?**

Our goal is not permanence but transformation. Lovefeeders exists only until abundance economies take hold — until food security is stable, and communities worldwide thrive on redefined value.

Our voice is one of many. The future is a choice of what we do today. We pray the doomsday scenarios aren’t realized. We hope the ease into good governance is peaceful. But just in case, we want to be ready, resilient, and adaptive.

Helping us succeed means helping us disappear. The faster we reach that finish line, the sooner the world transitions to resilience without us.
